# AI Usage Policy

R4Everyone has strict rules for the use of AI by outside contributors:

- **All AI usage must be disclosed.** State which AI tools you used (for
  example Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, or ChatGPT) and how much of
  the work they did. This applies to pull requests, issues, and discussions.

- **The human-in-the-loop must fully understand all code.** If you can't
  explain what your changes do and how they fit into the rest of R4Everyone
  without the help of an AI tool, don't submit them.

- **AI must not interact with the repository.** Only humans may open pull
  requests, file issues, post comments or reviews, add reactions, or take
  any other action on this repository. AI agents and bots acting on a
  contributor's behalf are not allowed. AI may help you write code or text,
  but a human must review it, edit it, and submit it themselves.

- **No AI-generated media is allowed.** This includes art, images, videos,
  audio, and any other media. Text and code are the only acceptable
  AI-generated content, subject to the rules above.

Contributions that don't follow these rules may be closed without review.

These rules apply only to outside contributors. Maintainers are exempt and
may use AI tools at their discretion.

## There Are Humans Here

R4Everyone is maintained by people. Every pull request, issue, and comment is
read and reviewed by a person, and reviewing work takes time. Submitting
low-effort or unverified AI output shifts the burden of checking it onto the
maintainers, which isn't fair to them.

AI tools can produce good work, but the quality depends on the person using
them. Until that's reliably the case, these rules exist to protect the
people who maintain this project.

## AI Is Welcome Here

This policy is not anti-AI. Maintainers use AI tools in their own work on
R4Everyone, and you're welcome to use them too, as long as you follow the
rules above. The problem isn't the tools; it's unreviewed work submitted by
people who don't understand it.
